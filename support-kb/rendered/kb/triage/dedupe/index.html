Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning can bring similar reports about different problems. Keep one ticket per supported cause within a product, with a count of unique report IDs.

The rule

A report is a duplicate when evidence supports the same cause, not the same subject (decision 0002, superseding the unchanged decision 0001):

  1. Read the body and confirm product, tenant, expected date, actual date, and entry channel.
  2. Look up the tenant’s locale and form version; browser locale and intake tags are guesses.
  3. For the exported date paths, compare the classifier’s cause with an open cause ticket.
  4. Merge only when the evidence agrees. If unknown or conflicting, keep separate and investigate.
  5. Deduplicate by report ID, preserve symptoms, and update the cause ticket’s count.
The desk serves every product. This explorer models the exported date paths only. A matching subject is not enough; two missing causes are not the same cause. See the #4471 review before routing these reports.

When you are unsure

Keep separate. Ask for tenant configuration, original input, expected/actual output, timezone, and a reproducible case. Preserve a suspected one-day shift even when the classifier proposes the parser group. Do not close a report merely because #4471 is marked known.

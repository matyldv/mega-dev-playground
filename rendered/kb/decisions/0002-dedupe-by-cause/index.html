0002 · Merge on supported cause, not subject

owner Codex (AI harness) accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Date: 28 October 2026 · Author: Codex, the student’s AI harness

Context

The #4471 review shows distinct causes behind similar subjects. Intake labels can disagree with tenant configuration. The desk still receives reports, so splitting historical reports alone will not prevent another incorrect merge.

Decision

Supersede decision 0001 without editing it. Keep one ticket per evidenced cause within a product. Read the body and establish the tenant locale, entry channel, form version, and expected/actual behavior. Use the tenant directory and the running classifier, not subject text or the browser’s intake label, to propose the cause.

Merge only when that evidence supports the same cause. A subject match is neither necessary nor sufficient. Unknown tenants, unsupported products, and conflicting or insufficient evidence stay separate for investigation; two unknown causes are not a match.

Consequences

  • Different subjects can join one cause ticket; identical subjects can stay apart.
  • Triage takes more reading, but does not bury a second fix under a known ticket.
  • Deduplicate export overlap by report ID and keep an auditable membership list.
  • Retain uncertainty and symptom notes; do not manufacture a third confirmed cause.
  • Review new reports and ticket membership as tenant configuration or evidence changes.

The triage guide implements this decision. The classifier only covers this export’s date paths; it is not a universal cause detector.
